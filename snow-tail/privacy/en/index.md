Hevin Mun (문해빈) ("we") operates the mobile game Snow Tail (the "Game"). This policy explains what data the Game processes and how you can control it. If this English version and the Korean version differ, the Korean version applies.

Effective date: 2026-11-17

## 1. Data we process and why

The Game has no account sign-up. We do not ask for your name, phone number, or email address.

| Data | When | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous player ID, authentication token | Created automatically on first launch | Online leaderboard access and record submission |
| The record name you enter, score, play statistics (snowmen, best combo, distance, section, play time, continues used), snowman layout, game mode and date, submission time | When you register a record that ranks | Showing the online leaderboard |
| Advertising ID (AAID/IDFA, if permitted), IP address, device information (model, OS, language), ad view and click data | When ads load or show | Serving ads, fraud prevention |
| Consent status | When you choose on the consent screen | Serving ads that match your choice |
| Purchase transaction ID, product ID | When you buy "Remove Ads" | Verifying and restoring the purchase |

- Registered records (name, score, play statistics, snowman layout) are **public to all players on the leaderboard.** Do not use a name that identifies you.
- Google Play and the App Store process payments. We never receive your card or other payment details.
- Your best score, last used name, and on-device record list stay on your device. They are not sent to us.

## 2. Retention

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Anonymous player ID | Until the service ends |
| Leaderboard records | While the record is in the top 100 of its leaderboard. Past "Today's Mountain" leaderboards are kept until the service ends. Deleted immediately when you delete them |
| Snowman layout | Deleted as soon as its record drops off the leaderboard |
| Advertising data | As set by each ad provider's policy |

When the retention period ends or we receive a deletion request, we delete the data without delay and in a way that cannot be recovered.

## 4. International transfers

While you play, the data in section 1 is sent over the network to the servers of the providers above, which may be in the United States or other countries. You can play offline to avoid this. Offline, the online leaderboard and ad-based continues are not available.

## 5. Personalized ads and how to opt out

Ad providers may use your advertising ID to show ads based on your interests.

- Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID
- iOS: Choose "Ask App Not to Track" when the Game asks, or turn tracking off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- In regions that require consent, use "Settings" (설정) on the Game's title screen to change your ad consent.

If you opt out, you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests.

## 6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your data at any time.

**Deleting your records**

1. Open the leaderboard screen in the Game.
2. Records you registered from this device show a trash icon. Tap it.
3. Tap "Delete" to confirm. The record and its snowman layout are deleted from the server immediately.

The server deletes a record only when the request comes from the same anonymous player that registered it, so nobody else can delete your records. If you uninstall the Game, your anonymous player ID is lost, and records registered before that can no longer be linked to you or deleted.

**Other requests**

For access, correction, or other requests, email hevinxx@gmail.com. We handle requests within 10 days of receipt and reply with the result.

Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game.

## 7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 14 (or the minimum age in your country). If we learn that we collected data from such a child, we delete it without delay.

## 8. Security

- All server communication is encrypted (HTTPS).
- Only the operator uses the server admin account, with two-factor authentication.
- Only server functions can write to leaderboards. Players can only read them.
